## Getting from the airport to the hotels (taxi)

Aruba's airport is Queen Beatrix International (AUA). Taxis in Aruba have no meters: rates are fixed
and should be checked in advance, and are given in Aruban florins and in US dollars.
Fares from the airport, before surcharges: Oranjestad $26; the low-rise hotel area $37; the high-rise
area, south $40 and north $41.
The minimum fare is $10.00. Four passengers ride for the base fare; each passenger after the fourth
adds $3; a taxi takes at most 7 passengers, not counting children under 2.
Sundays, official holidays, and every day from 11:00 pm to 7:00 am, there is a surcharge of $5.00.
Source: Aruba Tourism Authority, "Aruba Taxis & Limos", https://www.aruba.com/us/plan-your-visit/getting-around-aruba/taxis-limos (fare conditions at taxi.aw). Checked 22 Oct 2026.

## Money in Aruba (currency)

Aruba's currency is the Aruban florin (Afl.). Taxi rates are set at Afl. 1.80 to US$1.00.
The Central Bank of Aruba's daily rates for the US dollar on 22 Oct 2026: it buys banknotes at
Afl. 1.77 and transfers at Afl. 1.78, and sells at Afl. 1.80.
Source: Central Bank of Aruba, daily exchange rates, https://www.cbaruba.org/getExchangeRates.do?dispatch=daily; Aruba Tourism Authority taxis page (above). Checked 22 Oct 2026.

## Entering Aruba: passport, visa, ED card

A valid passport is required. American, Canadian, Caribbean, European and most South American
citizens do not need a visa to enter Aruba.
Every visitor is asked to fill in the Embarkation-Disembarkation (ED) card online before travelling:
it is Aruba's official online entry form, at https://www.edcardaruba.aw.
Travel insurance is not required, but recommended.
Aruba also has a sustainability fee; its amount is not stated on these pages, so it is not on file here.
Source: Aruba Tourism Authority, "Getting to Aruba", https://www.aruba.com/us/plan-your-visit/getting-to-aruba, and "Traveler health requirements", https://www.aruba.com/us/traveler-health-requirements. Checked 22 Oct 2026.

## The Circle's rules: points, fees, quotes, cancelling, leaving

Rules version: 1.0 (5 September 2026)
100 points are $1.00 of backing, fixed forever. The only fee is the Circle's 15% share, charged when
points are spent on a room, never when money is put in.
Base points never expire while a member is active or paused. Promotional points (tier bonus, streak,
founding) expire 24 months after they are issued.
No borrowing: when a quote is more than the points held, the difference is a cash top-up to the Banker.
A quote is locked for 72 hours; accepting it commits the points.
Cancelling follows the hotel's own terms, in points: what the hotel charges comes off; the rest comes
back. A refund from the hotel re-credits points, never cash.
Can I take my points out? Only by leaving: thirty days' notice, twelve months to use what you hold,
then base points are refunded at face value minus the $25 exit fee, within thirty days. Promotional
points are forfeited. Points and bookings cannot be sold or transferred.
A member's partner and children travel on their points at no extra charge; other guests use a guest
certificate or pay the same rate in cash.
Source: the Circle's rules page in the app (#/rules), version 1.0 of 5 September 2026; the $25 exit fee and 15% share from the live settings. Checked 22 Oct 2026.
