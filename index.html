What we will never do

The practices that gave this industry its reputation. If anyone claiming to represent us does any of these, it is not us.

  • Ask you to pay a fee before your property sells
  • Cold-call you claiming a buyer is already waiting

  • Charge you to "release" or "exit" your ownership

Straight answers

The questions everybody asks first

Is this another timeshare resale scam?

A fair question, and the reason this page exists. The scam pattern is always the same: someone contacts you, says a buyer is ready, and asks for a fee upfront. The sale never happens and the fee is gone.

We take nothing until your property actually sells, and the money moves through an Aruban notary rather than through us. If we never sell it, we are never paid.

What is my week actually worth?

Almost certainly less than you paid, and we would rather tell you that at the start than string you along. Resale value depends on the resort, the season, the unit size and whether the fees are current. High-season weeks at the beachfront resorts hold value best.

The valuation is free and comes with the comparable sales it is based on.

Can a foreigner own property in Aruba?

Yes. Aruba does not restrict foreign ownership, which is why so much of the island's resort property is owned from North America and Europe. Ownership can be freehold land or long-lease government land, and which one you are buying matters — we will tell you plainly which it is before you commit.

How long does a sale take?

Honestly: it varies, and anyone giving you a firm number is guessing. Finding the right buyer is the slow part; once an offer is accepted, the notary transfer is the predictable part. We will tell you what we are seeing in the market rather than what you want to hear.

What happens to my maintenance fees while it is listed?

They remain yours until the transfer completes, and they need to stay current — a week in arrears is much harder to sell and some resorts will block the transfer entirely. If you are behind, tell us; it is a common situation and it is workable.

Do you handle ordinary property too, not just timeshare?

Yes — condos, villas and land alongside resale weeks. The process and the fee model are the same: nothing upfront, commission on completion, transfer through the notary.
